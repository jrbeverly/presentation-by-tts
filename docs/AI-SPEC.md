# Sprint presentation compiler

Transform `data/sprint.json` into `data/presentation.yaml`. The result is a production brief for two consumers: a slide renderer and a narrator. It must stand on its own; neither consumer reads `data/sprint.json`.

## Editorial intent

- Tell one concise story: plan, delivery, evidence, learning, and what happens next.
- Use only facts present in the source. Do not invent status, causality, dates, or measurements.
- Put conclusions on slides and supporting explanation in narration. Do not narrate bullet text verbatim.
- Make every spoken number easy to hear. Expand ambiguous decimal notation in narration, but leave years and `YYYY.MM.DD` sprint labels as digits; the narrator corrects those to spoken form.
- Open with orientation and finish with decisions or next steps.

## Output contract

Emit valid YAML with this shape:

```yaml
presentation:
  title: string
  subtitle: string
  theme: acme-dark
  voice:
    model: full
    device: cpu
    profile: voice/karen-savage.json
    seed: integer
  slides:
    - id: stable-kebab-case-id
      layout: title | metrics | cards | flow | video | findings | comparison | next
      eyebrow: short section label
      title: concise assertion
      content: {}
      narration:
        - id: stable-cue-id
          target: slide-id or slide-id.element-id
          kind: speech | silence
          text: natural spoken script
          duration_seconds: number used only for silence
          pause_after_ms: integer
          audio: build/narration/NN-cue-id.wav
```

`content` is layout-specific structured data, never HTML. Use `lede`, `metrics`, `cards`, `steps`, `video`, `findings`, `comparison`, `goals`, and `footer` where they fit. Each item that narration may address should have a stable `id`. A `video` item names its derived media `source`, playback `duration_seconds`, caption, and optional callouts.

## Synchronization rules

- Every slide has at least one narration cue, and every cue has an audio path whose `NN` counts narration order across the whole deck, silence cues included. Omit `kind` for normal speech.
- `target` identifies the slide or `slide-id.element-id` being discussed; it must resolve to an ID in the same slide.
- Keep a thought within one cue. Use multiple cues when the speaker moves between distinct visual elements.
- Use a `silence` cue with `duration_seconds` when a video or other visual needs an intentional narration-free interval. Its WAV is generated by the build.
- Include the intended post-cue pause in `pause_after_ms`. The generated WAV contains that pause.
- Do not estimate timestamps. The build measures generated audio and derives cue and slide times from it.
- Slide order is YAML order. Narration order is slide order followed by cue order.

## Quality check

Before returning the YAML, verify that all source claims remain accurate, IDs are unique, cue targets resolve, audio filenames are unique, every slide advances the story, and the closing slide reflects the source capacity and goals.
